Reviews, tickets and plans, prepared by agents.

Concierge runs on your machine and drives AI agents for you. As a developer, they prepare your reviews, work through the feedback on your PRs and ship your Linear tickets. As a product manager, they break a brief down into a Linear project while you watch. You read the result and decide what goes out.

Modes

One workspace, for whoever you are today.

Work as a developer, a product manager, or both. Each mode has its own workspaces, skills and setup, and switching takes one click.

What it does

What Concierge does.

Each workspace in the app has its own colour, used here too.

As a developer

Reviews

When someone requests your review, an agent prepares it. You read the findings, then post or approve with one click.

Your PRs

On your own PRs, one click works through the review comments. Another pushes and replies to each thread.

Explain

What changes for users, and which systems it touches, drawn on a living map of the whole codebase.

Ship

A board of your Linear tickets. Pick a few, and agents ship them as stacked or parallel PRs.

Standup

What shipped, what's in review and what waits on you, gathered from the day's work.

Live activity

Every run shows what it's doing and what it costs. Answer all its questions in one go, and reach any tool with / in the box.

As a product manager

Plans

Write a brief, and an agent breaks it down with you into epics and user stories, drawn live as one roadmap to the goal. Play with it: try moves and plays, see the date move, then apply to Linear.

Your projects

Plan into a project you already have: its vision, tickets and timeline come in, with a forecast of when it lands. If you don't lead it, the plan only adds tickets.

Domain

Any PR or ticket in the business's own words: the parts it touches, its rules, and the user's journey before and after.

Planning

Make strategic moves, see what they change.

The plan is a roadmap you play with. Drag a ticket earlier, onto another epic, or into Later to cut it; every move is a scenario first, with when it lands, whether it fits the appetite and what finishes later, until you apply it, to Linear too.

Moves, tried first

Order, epic, priority, owner, or cut. The map shows where each moved ticket was, the critical path that sets the date, and the impact, until Apply.

Strategic plays

Fit the appetite, risk first, protect the date, balance owners: one click proposes the moves. Or ask the planner for an MVP slice, sequenced by value.

Ready for your company

Your regions, certifications and data turn into what each plan must cover: a DPIA, consent, change and security reviews, rollout, tests. Gaps a click from a ticket.

Filled for you

Your teams and everyone on them from Linear (people on several teams too), each one's pace, and what your code shows of GDPR, SOC 2 and the data you handle.

Best practices built in

User stories with acceptance criteria, MoSCoW and RICE on every ticket, now, next and later, and a risk register: the planner fills them, you decide.

Linear stays the source

Tickets live in Linear: the plan reads them, and writes back only what you apply. No second tracker.

Skills

Concierge only presents. Reviews, revisions and tickets run as your slash commands, and how it explains, briefs, plans and writes is a skill you pick for each. Use your team's own, or start from the author's set below.

/

Reviews someone else's PR in crit: findings first, posted only when you say.

 /

Works through the feedback on your PR: fixes, commits and drafted replies, pushed on your word.

 /

Ships a Linear ticket: root cause, the smallest fix that holds, checks, and a ready PR.

 /

The method behind every page that writes for you: one skill per feature, swapped for yours in Settings.

Agents

Each account signs in on its own. With several, Concierge spreads the work across them and moves to the next when one reaches its limit.

Fleet

Any number of machines.

Run it on as many machines as you have, all online at once: every one of them runs agents, and one is the main server your link opens. The standby follows the main server's state as it changes, over your tailnet: a run's update is a few kilobytes, never a file sync re-walking your disk. When the main server sleeps or goes offline, the standby takes over within minutes with every run, setting and review intact, and hands back once the first has caught up. A new machine joins with one command once it is on your tailnet: signed in as you, it is in at once. It is a private extension: ask the author for access.

Install

Getting started.

Clone and fill one file

Copy .env.example to .env, and set the folder with your clones and a GitHub token.

Start it

docker compose up -d, then open 127.0.0.1:4747. On Docker Desktop, turn on host networking first (Settings → Resources → Network).

Pick repos, sign in

A short setup signs you in to an agent, finds your repos, and clones the ones you don't have yet.

Terminal
$ gh repo clone ylkhayat/concierge && cd concierge
$ cp .env.example .env   # CODE_DIR and GITHUB_TOKEN
$ docker compose up -d

No Docker? Run ./install.sh instead.
